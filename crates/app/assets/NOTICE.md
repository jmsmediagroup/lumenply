# Bundled assets

## demo-photo.jpg

The photograph behind the demo document (`--demo`, File → Open demo
document, and the welcome screen's demo card).

- **Title:** Aoraki Sunrise — a mountain valley with a small lake in
  Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park, New Zealand
- **Author:** Aleks Dahlberg
- **Date taken:** 14 May 2017
- **Source:** <https://unsplash.com/photos/s5eCOoAWSaE>, via Wikimedia Commons:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aoraki_Sunrise_(Unsplash).jpg>
- **Licence:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication
  (<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>). The photo was
  published on Unsplash before 5 June 2017, while Unsplash still released
  photos under CC0; Commons records this with an archived copy of the
  original upload (Wayback Machine, 28 June 2017).
- **Changes:** downscaled from 3872 × 2592 to 1800 × 1205 (Lanczos) and
  re-encoded as JPEG quality 90, 4:4:4. The embedded profile was sRGB, so
  no colour conversion was needed and none is embedded.

No attribution is required under CC0; it is recorded here anyway.
